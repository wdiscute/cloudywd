# Selling Bin

Have you ever fished so much that you don't know what to do with all the fish you've caught? Time to send them off to the Selling Bin!

Put your fish in a selling bin and you'll get emeralds in exchange. Create the ultimate fish monopoly and control the market with the power of fish.

<div class="wk-warn">The selling bin comes from its own mod, <a href="/mods/wds-selling-bin/">wd's Selling Bin</a>, which is included with Starcatcher. Everything below is Starcatcher's default setup, and it can be customized: what can be sold, what it's worth and what you get paid with.</div>

## Fish Values

Every fish has a base value depending on its rarity. 100 value is worth one {{item minecraft:emerald}}, and 900 is worth a {{item minecraft:emerald_block}}.

| Rarity | Base value |
|---|---|
| <span class="wk-rarity common">Common</span> | 25 |
| <span class="wk-rarity uncommon">Uncommon</span> | 50 |
| <span class="wk-rarity rare">Rare</span> | 100 |
| <span class="wk-rarity epic">Epic</span> | 150 |
| <span class="wk-rarity legendary">Legendary</span> | 200 |

{{item pearl}} can also be sold, for 50.

## Bonuses

| Bonus | Effect |
|---|---|
| Size | Fish in the top 50% for size sell for more, up to double for the biggest ones. |
| Golden | Golden fish sell for 10 times their base value. |
| Quality | With Quality Food installed, iron, gold and diamond quality fish sell for 1.25x, 1.5x and 2x. |

# Baits

Baits are attached to your rod alongside your hook and bobber, and can be swapped using a [Tackle Box](tackle-boxes). Every bait makes fish bite faster, and most also come with their own modifier, such as attracting fish from a specific biome or letting you catch fish outside their usual conditions.

Unlike hooks and bobbers, a rod works fine without bait, but the right bait can make the fish you're after much easier to find.

## Worms

Worms can be found by using bone meal on farmland.

| Worm | Effect |
|---|---|
| {{item worm}} | Fish bite faster. |
| {{item almighty_worm}} | Fish bite faster, and fish are caught alive instead of as items. |
| {{item seeking_worm}} | Fish bite faster, and fish you haven't caught yet are more likely to bite. |

# Tournaments

Fishing tournaments let you put your skills to the test by competing against other anglers! Configure the tournament settings however you want and invite your friends over for a friendly fishing competition.

To get started, you'll just need a Tournament Stand.

## Hosting a Tournament

Use a Tournament Stand to open the tournament screen. As the organizer, you can name the tournament, set how long it lasts and choose how catches are scored. Hold Shift while changing a setting to change it 10 times faster.

Other players sign up at the same stand, and can withdraw before it starts. Once everyone is ready, press **Start**. Everyone signed up gets a message when the tournament starts and when it ends, along with the winner and their score.

While a tournament is running, press **Tab** to toggle the scoreboard. Past tournaments and their results can be looked up at any stand.

## Scoring

Only fish count towards your score. Each catch scores points based on its rarity, with bonuses on top:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Rarity points | Points for each catch, set separately for every rarity. By default, Common is worth 1 point, going up to 5 for Legendary. |
| Percentile Multiplier | Bonus points for bigger fish. |
| Perfect Catch Multiplier | Bonus points for a perfect catch. |

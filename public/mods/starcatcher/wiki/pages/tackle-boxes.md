# Tackle Boxes

Tackle boxes will be your best friend while fishing. They store your fish along with other fishing gear like hooks, bobbers and baits, and they're also where you swap the attachments on your rod.

Break a tackle box and it keeps everything inside, so you can carry your whole fishing setup with you. You may also crouch + right click to pick it back up!

## Tackle Box Boats

Tackle box boats are boats with a tackle box on the back, so your gear comes with you on the water. Sneak and use the boat to open its tackle box.

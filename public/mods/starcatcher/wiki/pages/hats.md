# Hats

Fisherman's hats are a stylish addition to any angler's outfit, and they're more than just cosmetic! Wear one on your head and its modifiers apply while you fish, just like a hook or bobber.

Each hat is crafted from a pearl, wool and carpet of its colour, and they can also be found rarely inside shipwreck chests.

## Effects

Every hat changes your fishing in its own way. Some make fishing easier, while others come with a downside in exchange for a big reward.

| Hat | Effect |
|---|---|
| {{item fisherman_hat_white}} | Fish are bigger and heavier. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_light_gray}} | An extra fish comes with your catch, but you can't see your catch while fishing and can't catch golden fish. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_gray}} | Perfect catches bring 3 extra fish, but you can't see your catch, can't catch golden fish, and it greatly increases the penalty for missing a sweet-spot. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_black}} | 5 extra fish come with your catch, but you can't see your catch, can't catch golden fish, and the minigame is much harder. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_brown}} | Your bobber flies really far. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_red}} | Fish sometimes ignore their time-of-day restrictions, and sweet-spots flip every time you hit one. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_orange}} | Adds a Glowing sweet-spot. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_yellow}} | Perfect catches are more likely to become golden, but fish take longer to bite and it increases the penalty for missing a sweet-spot. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_lime}} | Fish bite much faster, but an ill-tempered friend might show up. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_green}} | Fish are easier to reel in and give extra experience, but you can't catch epic or legendary fish. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_cyan}} | Fish swim away slower, and it decreases the penalty for missing a sweet-spot. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_light_blue}} | Adds a Dripstone sweet-spot, but fish take longer to bite and missing a sweet-spot freezes your handle. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_blue}} | Fish sometimes ignore their weather restrictions, sweet-spots flip every time you hit one, and the handle is slower. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_purple}} | Slows down the handle. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_magenta}} | Speeds up the handle. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_pink}} | The handle is tremendously fast and missing sets it on fire, and you can only catch trash. |

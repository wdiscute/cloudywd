# Golden Fish

Every fish has a super rare golden variant. Golden fish shine with a golden glow, sell for 10 times their usual value at the [Selling Bin](selling-bin), and count towards some of the [Trophies](trophies).

Each fish can only be caught golden once. After you've caught a fish's golden variant, that fish will never come up golden again for you, so every golden catch is a new one for your collection.

## Base Chance

Every catch has a **1%** chance to be golden. A perfect catch rolls an extra **1%** on top.

Each source of golden chance below rolls separately, so stacking several of them adds up.

## Increasing the Chance

| {{item gold_hook}} | +5% on every catch. |
| {{item golden_bobber}} | +5% on every catch. |
| {{item fisherman_hat_yellow}} | +5% on perfect catches, but fish take longer to bite and it increases the penalty for missing a sweet-spot. |

Golden worms can be found by using bone meal on farmland. |
| <a class="wk-chip" href="https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherite_Upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span class="wk-icon" style="background-image:url(/mods/starcatcher/wiki/data/icons/minecraft/netherite_upgrade_smithing_template.png)"></span><span>Netherite upgrade</span></a> | Upgrading your rod with a {{item minecraft:netherite_upgrade_smithing_template}} gives +10% on perfect catches, along with lava fishing. |

With everything combined, the best total chance is about **19.6%** on a normal catch and **31.9%** on a perfect catch.

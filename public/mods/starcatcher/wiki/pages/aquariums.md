# Aquariums

Aquariums are a great addition to any home, and are sure to make your living space come alive! Place aquarium blocks next to each other and they connect into one big tank, so you can build it in any shape and size.

They can be customized with ground blocks and decorations to fit any setting and any style.

## Adding Fish

Use a bucket while fishing to catch a bucket of fish, then use it on an aquarium to release the fish inside. Dead fish don't go in an aquarium!

## Ground & Decorations

Use a ground block on an aquarium to give it a floor, then use a decoration on it to place it on top. Crouch and right click with an empty hand to remove the decoration, then the ground.

| Type | Items |
|---|---|
| Ground | Cobblestone, Mossy Cobblestone, Mossy Stone Bricks, Deepslate, Moss Block, Amethyst Block, any Wool, any Terracotta |
| Plants | Kelp, Seagrass, Sea Pickle. Use bone meal on kelp and seagrass to grow them. |
| Coral | Every coral and coral fan, alive or dead |
| Other | Amethyst buds and clusters, Torch |

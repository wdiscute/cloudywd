# Trophies

Trophies show off your skill as an angler, and are sure to put others to shame for their lack of trophyfulness.

You don't craft trophies, you fish them up. As soon as you meet a trophy's requirements, it will bite your hook on your next catch. Each trophy can only be caught once.

## Requirements

| Trophy | Requirement |
|---|---|
| {{item trophy_copper}} | Catch 25 fish. |
| {{item trophy_iron}} | Catch 35 fish. |
| {{item trophy_gold}} | Catch 100 fish, 2 different legendary fish and 2 different golden fish. |
| {{item trophy_emerald}} | Catch 200 fish and 10 different legendary fish. |
| {{item trophy_diamond}} | Catch 500 fish and 10 different golden fish. |
| {{item trophy_of_the_older_angler}} | Catch every fish. |

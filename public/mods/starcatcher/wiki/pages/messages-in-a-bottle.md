# Messages In A Bottle

Every so often you may come across long forgotten stories stuck inside a bottle, drifting amid the waves. Whoever left them is most likely gone by now, but you too can write down your stories in a letter, put it inside a bottle, and throw it out to the ocean!

## Sending a Message

Craft a {{item letter}} and write your message in it. Then combine it with a glass bottle to make a {{item bottled_letter}}, and throw it into the sea to send it on its journey. Just dropping it won't get it far!

## Finding a Message

A {{item message_in_a_bottle}} is fished up when another player throws a bottled letter into the sea, or from one of the secret messages in a bottle built into Starcatcher.

Throw it to break it open. You'll get the {{item message}} inside, along with a {{item broken_bottle}}, which can be thrown at entities to damage them.

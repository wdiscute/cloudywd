# Fish Plaques

Fish plaques let you hang your best catches on the wall. Place a plaque on any wall, then right click it with a fish to mount it. Right click it again to take the fish back.

Breaking a plaque drops the fish on it, so you'll never lose a catch.

## Crafting

Every plaque is crafted the same way: a log surrounded by 8 sticks. The log's wood type decides the plaque.
